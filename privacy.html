Gun Keep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Overview

Gun Keep is a personal firearm inventory and record-keeping application. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use Gun Keep.

Your Gun Keep Records

Information you enter into Gun Keep, including firearm, ammunition, parts, purchase, sale, shooting journal, photo, and related records, is stored on your device.

Gun Keep does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the application.

Advertising

Gun Keep uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google's advertising services may automatically collect certain information from your device and your use of the application.

Depending on device settings, location, consent, and applicable law, this information may include device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, advertising information, application interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

Information collected by Google is handled according to Google's privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

Gun Keep offers an optional in-app purchase to remove advertising. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Gun Keep does not receive or store your payment card information.

Photos and Camera

Gun Keep allows you to associate photos with your records. Photos selected or captured for use in Gun Keep are used to provide this functionality and are stored as part of your app data.

Children

Gun Keep is not directed to children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
